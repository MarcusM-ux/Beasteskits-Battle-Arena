BEASTESKITS BATTLE ARENA

Real-time 1v1 creature battles.
Two players. One keyboard.
Outplay your opponent to win.

CONTROLS

Player 1 (Left Side)
Keys To Move: W A S D
Keys To Attack: Q E Z X Special Key: R
Player 2 (Right Side)
Kets To Move: Arrow Keys
Keys To Attack: O P K L Special Key: M

MOVEMENT COMES FIRST

Use your movement keys to position your creature.
Good movement wins fights.

ATTACKING

Use your attack keys to strike your opponent.
Each attack has different timing and range.

HEALTH & WINNING

Hit your opponent to lower their health.
Reduce their health to zero to win the battle!

REAL-TIME COMBAT

This is not turn-based.
Timing, spacing, and positioning matter.
If your opponent spams, use your moves to get the better of them!

READY?
